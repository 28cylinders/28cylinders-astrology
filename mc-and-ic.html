What Are Your MC and IC?

Your MC is the roof. Your IC is what holds it up.

October 27, 2026

In shortYour MC is the top of your birth chart: career, reputation, what you are known for. Your IC is the bottom: home, family, roots. They are always opposite signs, and career fulfillment tends to come when both are fed.

You know your Sun, Moon, and Rising. Two more points sit on the same axis, and they carry a lot of weight when it comes to work, money, and what you're actually here to build: the MC and the IC.

Picture your chart as a circle. Your Rising sign sits on the left horizon. The MC (Medium Coeli, Latin for "middle of the sky") is the very top of the chart, where the Sun would sit at noon on your birthday. The IC (Imum Coeli, "bottom of the sky") is directly opposite, the point beneath your feet at midnight. They are always exactly 180° apart, so one tells you the other: an MC in Capricorn means an IC in Cancer.

The MC: what you're known for

The MC is the public side of the chart. It's traditionally tied to career, reputation, and calling. Less "what's your job title" and more "what do you want to be recognized for, and what does fulfilling work feel like to you?" The sign on your MC, and any planets sitting near it, color how you earn, how you lead, and how the world sees your work.

The IC: what you stand on

The IC is the private side: home, family, ancestry, and your emotional base. It's where you feel safe, what you inherited (the good and the complicated), and what you return to when nobody's watching.

The IC is the foundation for the MC. A tall building needs deep footings. Whatever your IC says you need to feel secure is often what makes your MC ambition sustainable instead of exhausting.

Take a Capricorn MC, driven, structured, built for status and long-term achievement. Its IC is Cancer, which needs emotional safety, belonging, and a place to actually rest. Chasing the Capricorn goals while starving the Cancer needs is a classic recipe for burnout. The reverse is true too: someone who nests at the IC and never lets the MC out may feel safe but quietly unfulfilled.

Balance, not a ranking

Neither point wins. Career fulfillment, and money that actually feels good to make, seems to show up when both ends of the axis are fed. Some people lead with the MC and circle back to build their foundation later; others build the foundation first. The better question isn't "which one am I?" but "which one am I starving right now?"

Quick answers

What is the MC in astrology?

The MC (Medium Coeli, or Midheaven) is the highest point of your birth chart, where the Sun would sit at noon on your birthday. It is traditionally tied to career, reputation, and calling.

What is the IC in astrology?

The IC (Imum Coeli) is the lowest point of your birth chart, directly opposite the MC. It represents home, family, roots, and your private emotional base.

Are the MC and IC always opposite signs?

Yes. They are always exactly 180 degrees apart, so one tells you the other. An MC in Capricorn means an IC in Cancer.

Is the MC the same as the 10th house?

Often they line up, but not always. In many house systems the MC marks the start of the 10th house. In Whole Sign houses, the MC is a specific degree that can fall in a different house than the 10th.

How do I find my MC and IC?

You need your birth date, exact birth time, and birth place. The MC moves about one degree every four minutes, so an exact time matters. Use the calculator on this page, or the full birth chart calculator.
